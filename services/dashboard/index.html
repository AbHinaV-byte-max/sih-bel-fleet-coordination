How the Autonomous Fleet Coordinator Works

A quick 1-minute visual guide for new users.

1

Decentralized Peer-to-Peer AI

Every robot runs its own independent navigation and decision agent. There is no central computer that can fail or freeze the warehouse.

2

Deterministic Right-of-Way

When two robots cross paths, they broadcast intent waypoints. A deterministic right-of-way protocol (Priority → Closer to Goal → ID) ensures zero collisions without blind stops.

3

Hungarian Task Matching

New delivery orders are automatically matched to the most capable, closest available robot using the minimum-cost Hungarian algorithm.

4

Realistic Physics & 3D Graphics

Toggle between the crisp 2D Top-Down map and the realistic 3D Isometric view. Robots simulate chassis acceleration tilt, steering inertia, and suspension vibration.
